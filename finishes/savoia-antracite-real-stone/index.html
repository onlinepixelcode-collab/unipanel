Care · Real Stone

  1. 01Dust with a soft, dry brush or cloth to keep the mineral texture clean.
  2. 02Spot-clean with a lightly damp cloth; do not soak the surface.
  3. 03Avoid abrasive pads and acidic household cleaners.

An all-inclusive cleaning solution is available for each product. Ask your showroom for the right one.
